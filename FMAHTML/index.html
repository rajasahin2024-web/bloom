Where Bloom came from

Founded in 2025, in three countries at once.

Bloom began as three separate teaching practices — one in Muscat, one in Milan and one in Shanghai — run by examiners who kept meeting the same problem: students who understood English but could not show it under exam conditions.

In 2025 the three merged and moved their operations to Bengaluru, India, where the academy is now headquartered. The teaching still runs out of all four locations, which is why there is almost always a live class in your evening.

How we do it

Five stages. Nothing is guessed.

Most preparation fails for one reason: students practise without ever checking what they got wrong. Bloom is built to make that impossible.

Placement test

Twenty-five minutes across grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, writing and speaking. It returns a CEFR level, a band per skill, and your strongest and weakest areas — from what you actually did, not from what you told us.

Your English profile

An honest picture: where you sit on the CEFR scale, what your estimated band is on every exam scale, and which first-language errors we will be watching for as a speaker of your language.

A plan built from it

A day-by-day schedule to your test date, weighted towards your weakest skill, with a mock every Saturday and a checkpoint every fortnight. Tick each block off as you do it.

Practice with real feedback

Write an essay, get a band against all four descriptors and every error explained. Record a cue card, get fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation scored separately. Your recurring mistakes are collected into an error bank.

Measured, then adjusted

Every scored activity moves your estimate. When a mock says your reading is the problem, next week's plan changes. You always know what to revise today and what you have already mastered.

The difference

Feedback is the whole product.

Anyone can sell you practice papers. Half a band takes roughly a hundred focused hours — and those hours only count if something tells you, precisely, what went wrong.

  • Every essay marked against the four official criteriaTask Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy — separately, with a band each.
  • Sentence-level corrections, not a gradeThe wrong version, the right version, and why. Repeat offenders go into your personal error bank.
  • Errors targeted to your first languageArticles for Hindi and Urdu speakers, plural and tense endings for Mandarin speakers, final consonants for Vietnamese speakers.
  • Spaced repetition that decides for you570 academic words scheduled so the ones you miss come back tomorrow and the ones you know disappear for a month.

What the partnership covers

Everything between your result and your first lecture.

  • Requirement checking before you sitWe confirm the exact overall and per-skill minimum your shortlist demands, so you do not pass the test and still miss the offer.
  • Credential evaluationDegree and transcript assessment for the UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia, Germany and Italy — including ECTS conversion.
  • Pathway and foundation routesIf your band is not there yet, a pre-sessional English or foundation route that still leads to the same degree.
  • Scholarship and funding searchMerit awards, regional bursaries and assistantships matched to your profile — most students never find these alone.
  • Visa filing and interview prepFinancial evidence, the credibility interview, and the English-language part of the visa rules that changes most often.
  • One file, both teamsYour Bloom band history, mock reports and score conversions attach to your FMA application automatically.
