Frequently Asked Questions
How long is the course duration?
The MedItalia Plus+ programme runs for three months, typically from May through July, aligning with the IMAT preparation calendar. You retain access to all recordings and materials until your exam date.
Are all IMAT subjects covered?
Yes. Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, and Critical Thinking & Problem Solving are all covered in depth through 144 hours of live instruction, topic-wise quizzes, and full simulation practice.
Will I receive personal guidance?
Every student receives a personalised study planner, continuous performance tracking, individual feedback sessions after mock tests, and ongoing access to instructor support throughout the programme.
Are sessions recorded?
All live lectures are recorded and uploaded to the student portal. You may revisit any session at any time up until your IMAT exam — no class is ever permanently missed.
What do the mock tests involve?
The programme includes 12 full MedItalia simulations, 12 archived 2025/26 batch papers, 6 minor simulations, and 6 February session simulations. All tests are ranked so you can benchmark against peers and track progress in real time.
How do I enrol?
Click "Enrol Now via WhatsApp" and our team will guide you through registration and payment directly. The process is straightforward, and all onboarding support is provided via WhatsApp.
Is a certificate issued upon completion?
Yes. A certificate of completion is awarded to all students who successfully complete the programme requirements. This is included in your enrolment at no additional cost.
