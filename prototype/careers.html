Why Bloom

What the job is actually like.

No inflated claims. This is how the academy runs, and the parts people tell us they value.

You specialise

Nobody here teaches all four skills. You own one part of the exam and become the person the others come to about it.

Small cohorts

Ten to eighteen students, never more. You know every name and every weakness by week two.

48-hour marking

It is a standard, not an aspiration. In return, marking time is timetabled — you are not doing it at midnight.

Four time zones

Bengaluru, Muscat, Milan and Shanghai. Remote and hybrid contracts are normal, not a concession.

Recognition on record

Best Faculty is quarterly and measured on student band gain, not popularity. It comes with a certificate and a revision.

Paid development

DELTA, examiner training and conference attendance are funded, with study leave to sit the exams.

Open positions

Roles we are hiring for.

Apply below and your application enters our hiring pipeline the same minute. We read every one, and reply either way.

How hiring works

Six stages, no ghosting.

You will know which stage you are at, and roughly how long the next one takes.

1
Applied We confirm receipt the same day.
2
Screening A look at your experience and qualifications. Two to three days.
3
Demo lesson Teaching roles only. Forty-five minutes with real students.
4
Interview With the Operational Leader and the relevant department head.
5
Offer Written offer, terms and start date. Valid fourteen days.
6
Onboarding Contract, induction, and a named mentor for your first term.
