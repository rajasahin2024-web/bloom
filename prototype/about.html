About Bloom

Founded because good students kept failing for bad reasons.

Bloom started in 2025 as three separate teaching practices — one in Muscat, one in Milan, one in Shanghai — each run by former IELTS examiners who had spent years watching capable candidates lose marks on things nobody had ever told them about: a missing article, a date written the wrong way, thirty seconds of silence in Part 2.

The three merged that same year and moved operations to Bengaluru. Across 2,840 students the method has not changed. Test honestly. Teach the specific thing. Mark it against the real criteria. Repeat until the number moves.

What we believe
  • An honest level beats a flattering oneA placement test that tells you what you want to hear produces a study plan that wastes your time.
  • Practice without feedback is not practiceIt is the single biggest waste of preparation time we see, and it is why most self-study plateaus.
  • Your first language predicts your errorsNot your ability. Targeting interference patterns is faster than general grammar revision.
  • Consistency beats intensityTwenty focused minutes a day beats four hours on a Sunday. The streak is the best predictor we have.
  • We will tell you if you are not readySitting the test too early costs money and confidence. We would rather lose the entry fee than your trust.
