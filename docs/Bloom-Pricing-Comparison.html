5. How we can price this way — honestly

  • Your prototype removed the riskiest phase. Every screen and module is already designed and agreed — we quote for build, not for months of discovery and re-design.
  • Compact senior team, no layers. One architect/PM, one application engineer, one DevOps+AI engineer — no account-management overhead baked into the price.
  • Parallel AI track. The AI Tutor is built alongside the platform on a second track, so the timeline stays 3 months instead of stretching to 6+.
  • Reusable internal components. Auth, CMS, billing, and notification plumbing come from our tested internal modules — we don't bill you to reinvent them.
  • Milestone-based contract. Payments map to accepted deliveries (website → admin → teachers → student + go-live), so you only pay for work that has been accepted.
